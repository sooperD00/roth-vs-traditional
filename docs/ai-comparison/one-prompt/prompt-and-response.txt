The app takes one position and builds everything around it: Traditional-vs-Roth is a single bet — your marginal rate now vs. in retirement — and everything else is a tiebreaker.

So: pick your two rates from bracket chips, watch the same dollar walk both timelines side by side, and get a verdict stamped (literally — it's rendered as a tax stamp) with the dollar difference. Equal rates gets an honest "DEAD HEAT" (the math is commutative), which hands you off to the tiebreaker list: Roth's effective-limit advantage, RMDs, splitting as a real strategy, income limits.

Defaults I chose for you to argue with: 22% now, 12% later, $10k, 25 years, 7% growth.
